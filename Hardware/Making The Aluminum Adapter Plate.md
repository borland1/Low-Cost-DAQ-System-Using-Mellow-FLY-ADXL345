Here's a helper Guide for making the Aluminum Adapter Plate

<img width="714" height="712" alt="image" src="https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/549b3760-2c0c-486e-9f35-da1ac448b74d" />

First print out the Adapter Plate template from the PDF file 'MagneticBasePlateTemplate.pdf' found in the 'Hardware' Folder here.
Be sure to adjust the printer settings to print 'Actual Size'.

I used a hacksaw to hand cut the aluminum square plate to size,
then cleaned up the edges with a hand file.

Use a pair of scissors to cutout the paper template and attach it to the aluminum square plate.
I used Rubber Cement as a quick drying adhesive, however double sided tape will work just as well.

Center tap the three holes using a sharp nail or brad and small hammer.
Then use a standard center tap to prepare for centering the drill bit.

To tap the M3x0.5mm threads, you'll need a 2.50mm drill bit. For best results, make
sure you have the drill orientated vertically, then drill a slightly smaller pilot hole first
before finishing with the 2.50mm drill.
